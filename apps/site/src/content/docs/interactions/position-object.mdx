**3.2.16 Position Object Interaction** — a single image (the object) that the candidate places on another image (the stage), once or several times. The response has base type `point`: for each placement, where the object's centre point landed, in the stage image's own coordinates.

Also see the attributes shared by every interaction on the [overview page](/interactions/overview/).

## Example

<InteractionDemo>
  <qti-position-object-stage>
    <img src="https://qti-convert.web.app/images/uk.png" alt="Map of the United Kingdom" width="206" height="280" />
    <qti-position-object-interaction response-identifier="RESPONSE" max-choices="3">
      <img src="https://qti-convert.web.app/images/airport.png" alt="Airport" width="30" height="30" />
    </qti-position-object-interaction>
  </qti-position-object-stage>
</InteractionDemo>

```html
<qti-position-object-stage>
  <img src="uk.png" alt="Map of the United Kingdom" width="206" height="280" />
  <qti-position-object-interaction response-identifier="RESPONSE" max-choices="3">
    <img src="airport.png" alt="Airport" width="30" height="30" />
  </qti-position-object-interaction>
</qti-position-object-stage>
```

The stage holds the stage image and one or more interactions; each interaction holds its object. The object sits beside the stage, and every placed copy is drawn on the stage image.

## Attributes

| Attribute      | Type     | Default              | Notes                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | -------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `max-choices`  | `number` | `1`                  | Most placements; `0` = no limit. At the maximum no further copy is placed and the host carries `:state(used)`.   |
| `min-choices`  | `number` | `0`                  | Fewest placements for a valid response.                                                                          |
| `center-point` | `string` | centre of the object | The object's centre point, `x y` from its top-left, in the object's own pixels. The response records this point. |
| `response`     | `string` | —                    | Points as `x y,x y`, to restore a response.                                                                      |

## Placing

- **Pointer:** drag the object onto the stage, or click it and then click the stage. Drag a placed copy to move it, or off the stage to remove it.
- **Keyboard:** focus the object and press Enter or Space to place a copy at the centre of the stage. On a placed copy, the arrow keys move it one unit (with Shift, ten) and Delete or Backspace removes it.

Coordinates are in the stage image's `width`/`height` (or its natural size), so a stage shown smaller or larger records the same points. Area-mapping areas are never shown, only the candidate's placements.

## Styling

Parts: `object` (tokens `selected` while a click has picked it up, `used` at `max-choices`), `point` (each placed copy), `message`. States: `used`, `disabled`, `readonly`.
