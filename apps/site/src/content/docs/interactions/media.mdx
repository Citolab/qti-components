**[3.2.15 Media Interaction](https://www.imsglobal.org/spec/qti/v3p0/impl#h.u9utgghwf8ck)** — gives more control over how the candidate interacts with a time-based media object, and reports how many times it was played as the response.

Also see the attributes shared by every interaction on the [overview page](/interactions/overview/).

## Example

<InteractionDemo>
  <qti-media-interaction response-identifier="RESPONSE">
    <qti-prompt>Play this video.</qti-prompt>
    <video width="320" height="240" controls>
      <source src="/assets/qti-media-interaction/earth.mp4" type="video/mp4" />

    </video>
  </qti-media-interaction>

## `qti-media-interaction`

| Attribute | Type | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `step` | `number` | `10` | Per the QTI spec, the required minimum play count — this component reads the attribute but doesn't itself enforce it as a hard minimum. |

The interaction looks for its first `<audio>`, `<video>`, or `<object>` child (in that priority order) and increments its response every time that element fires a native `ended` event.
