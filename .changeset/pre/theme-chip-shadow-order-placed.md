Four optional tier-1 inputs, so a brand can be made of tokens alone: `--cito-shadow-chip` sets a chip's resting lift (the shadow `--cito-depth` works out otherwise), and `--cito-color-order-placed`, `--cito-color-order-placed-content` and `--cito-color-order-placed-border` give an order interaction's placed chip a look of its own, and `--cito-color-well` fills a `.qti-well` (the base-200 tint otherwise). Unset, nothing changes.
